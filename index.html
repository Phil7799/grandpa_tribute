❖  His Story  ❖

A Life Well Lived

1943 — Birth & Family

Born of Kochieng East

The late Mzee Hannington Awino Osango was born in 1943, the first-born son to the late Mzee Samson Osango Awino and Mama Priscilla Wanyori Osango of Kochieng East location, Kadibo division, Nyando sub-county, Kisumu county. He was step-son to the late Esther Ocholla Osango.

In 1968, he was joined in traditional marriage to Mama Anjelina Oriema Awino. God blessed their union with five children: the late Benter Ouma, the late Calvins Otieno, Lilian Otieno, Hon. George Wanyori, and instructor Samson Osango. He was father-in-law to Mary Wanyori and the late Dorine Osango and Beatrice Otieno, and to his son-in-law George Otieno Masore.

Mzee was blessed with 17 grandchildren — Phillip, Hannington Jr. (Reinhard), Vincent, Angeline, Samson, Felix, Tonny, Samson Wanyori, Penny, Hannington James, Hailey, Keyden, Noelah, Patience, the late Caylon and Ariana — and great-grandchildren Jay, Bonny, Angeline, Tatiana, Tina and Jordyn. He was a devoted brother, brother-in-law, and a loving uncle to a wide family of nephews, nieces and grandnephews too many to count — and cherished every one of them.

Education & Work

The Carpenter's Craft

Hanningtone began his education at Kisumu Union School, laying the foundation of his academic journey, before proceeding to Rabuor Intermediate Primary School to continue his studies.

After school, Mzee ventured into the construction industry, working with various companies across Mombasa, Nairobi, Kisumu and Siaya, among other places. Through dedication, skill and years of experience, he rose to become a senior carpenter — hardworking, skilled, and committed, taking great pride in the work that provided for and supported his family.

Christian Life

A Cheerful Giver

Hanningtone will be remembered as a man whose life was deeply rooted in faith, love, humility and service to others — a testimony to the importance of family, friendship, and living in harmony with those around him.

A staunch Christian, a virtue inherited from his parents, he participated in all church activities. Being a generous carpenter, he freely offered his skill to put up the roof of the New St. Meshack's Central Ranjira Church, where he was a devoted member. What a cheerful giver — Ranjira Church has lost one of its own.

Social Life

Storyteller, Music-Lover, Family Man

Hanningtone was a loving, caring and generous man whose warm heart touched the lives of many. He was welcoming to everyone, always ready to share what he had with family, friends and visitors.

He had a special love for children and took great joy in watching his family grow, often encouraging his sons and daughters toward a large and united family — a blessing, in his view. A God-fearing man of good morals, he loved music and found great joy sharing it with others. He was also a gifted storyteller, and his stories, wisdom and humor brought people together and created lasting moments.

He will be remembered for his kindness, generosity, love for family, welcoming spirit, and the joy he brought to those around him. His legacy of love, faith and togetherness lives on in the hearts of his children, grandchildren, relatives, friends and the wider community.

❖  The Final Chapter  ❖

Sickness & Homegoing

  • Early 2026

    Having enjoyed good health for most of his life and remaining strong and active, Mzee began experiencing health challenges — chest pain and a severe cough prompted the family to seek medical attention.

  • District Hospital, Kisumu

    He was admitted and remained in hospital for one week. After showing signs of improvement, he was discharged and returned home to continue his recovery.

  • Relapse

    His condition deteriorated again and he was rushed back to the same health facility. Following further tests, he was diagnosed with severe pneumonia, and subsequently developed deep vein thrombosis (DVT), which resulted in a blood clot.

  • Continued Decline

    Mzee's health continued to decline as he also developed meningitis. Despite the tireless efforts of the medical team, the loving care of his family, and the prayers of relatives and friends, his condition became increasingly severe.

  • Wednesday, 29th July 2026

    Mzee succumbed peacefully, surrounded by the love of the family he had spent his life building.
